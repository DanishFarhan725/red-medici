# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + vanilla TypeScript + GSAP (ScrollTrigger). Builds to static files hostable on any static host (Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages). User asked for "whatever is more professional and suitable for online website hosting".

## Users

Gamers and active, sport-minded listeners evaluating a new premium true-wireless earbud at launch. They arrive from social or ads, scan the product visually first, and decide whether to join the waitlist.

## Product Purpose

Red Medici is a futuristic true-wireless earbud set positioned for sports and gaming. The site is its launch page: make the industrial design legible and desirable, state the specs and price, and collect waitlist sign-ups.

## Positioning

Angular, faceted metallic-red shells with black internals, white silicone tips and an illuminated white light bar on each bud: the product reads as an engineered object, not a lifestyle accessory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Specs and price are placeholders authored for launch at the user's request ("futuristic sports and gaming set, give it specs and price accordingly"). They live in one data file so they can be replaced with real figures. [inferred, user-delegated]
- Waitlist form validates client-side only; no backend yet.
- Single long-scroll page.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Red Medici.
- Palette pinned by brief: porcelain white #F8F8F6, light grey #E9E9E7, graphite #242424, crimson #D71920 (red used sparingly for CTAs/accents).
- Typography direction chosen by user: serif display headlines with monospaced technical labels.
- Avoid: excessive gradients, glassmorphism, glowing borders, blobs, repetitive cards, gratuitous rounding, generic slogans, emoji icons, fake statistics.
- No fake 3D.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/media/hero.webp` — product photo, two earbuds on white.
- `public/media/hover.mp4` — Gemini-generated clip of the earbuds hovering in the air (user: "the earbuds only hover in the air in the background").
- No other imagery, no reviews, press, or testimonials. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The earbuds are always the focal point.
2. Show the object; let detail crops do the persuading.
3. Every number on the page is editable data, never decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-complete, reduced-motion respected (video paused, scroll scrubs disabled).
